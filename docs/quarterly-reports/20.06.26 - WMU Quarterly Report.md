**Department of Energy Project: Quarterly Report**

**June 26th, 2020**

Our team has developed a validated compressed natural gas (CNG) fuel economy model of a BRT (Bus Rapid Transit) bus in the FastSim python version. A Conventional technology bus is selected as a baseline bus for the evaluation of performance to provide the best possible comparison with Optimal EMS and ultimately an integrated Optimal TMS bus model. The baseline bus selected is a 60-foot NABI. FastSim is a backward-looking model with knowledge of the vehicle state. It calculates the power required to achieve that state. Inputs to the model are vehicle speed, road grade, and the data sampling increment (time step). All vehicle data can be imported automatically, unless the vehicle manufacture model is not in the database. For this case parameters should be modified to represent the desired vehicle. The BRT model is validated based on measured fuel economy over the 20 hour real world drive cycle in Fort Collins. Key parameters such as drag coefficient, vehicle override kg, wheel rolling resistance coefficient, axillary power and idle fuel consumption are tuned to fit the model with the measured data from the physical tests.
